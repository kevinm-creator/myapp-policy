Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: November 26, 2026

GoSki is operated by RAK Industries LLC, a Connecticut company. This policy explains what the GoSki app collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account: your name, email address and account ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they share your name and email address with us (or, with Hide My Email, an Apple relay address). Google also shares a link to your profile picture, which is kept with your sign-in record but not used.

Your settings: favorite resorts, resort requests, ski regions, ski passes and alert choices, including your time zone for quiet hours.

Alerts: your phone's notification token if you turn on App Notifications, and your mobile phone number and a record of your consent if you choose text alerts.

Purchases: purchase and subscription records from the App Store, Google Play or Galaxy Store, through RevenueCat. The stores handle payment; we never receive your card number.

App use: how you use some screens, such as the GoSki PRO page, and anonymous totals such as the number of ski days recorded each day and, on Android, how many installs came from our ads or links (Google Play tells the app which link was tapped before installing; only the daily totals are kept).

Location: when you start a ski session and allow location access, GoSki records your precise GPS location and barometer readings to measure your route, speed and elevation. Recordings stay encrypted on your phone; we don't upload them.

2. How We Use It

To run your account and the app's features, send the alerts you ask for, verify purchases, prevent abuse and improve the app. We don't sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

Service providers that run GoSki for us (hosting, purchase validation, email, text-message and notification delivery, maps and weather) receive only what they need to provide their service, and must protect it. Map and weather services receive the map areas you view. Apple, Google and Samsung handle store purchases and sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Ad measurement: outside Europe, GoSki uses Meta's (Facebook's) app software to tell Meta when the app is installed or opened and when you start a GoSki PRO trial or purchase, so we can count installs from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta also receives basic device information, an app-created ID, your IP address and, on Android, your advertising ID, and uses it under its own privacy policy. GoSki doesn't ask to track you on iPhone, so Meta doesn't get your iPhone's advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Privacy, then Ads). We share nothing else with advertising companies.

Text messages: we do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your consent to receive text messages with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the providers that deliver our messages.

4. Keeping and Deleting Data

We keep your information until you delete your account. Deleting it in Settings removes your account and the data stored with it, and erases the recordings saved on that phone, so export any you want first. Records of the alerts and notifications we send are kept for about 90 days. To prevent sign-up abuse, we keep a scrambled copy of a phone number for up to 30 days after we text it a code, even if the account is deleted. Purchase records stay with the stores and RevenueCat, and deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. You can also ask us by email to delete your data.

5. Your Choices

You can change your settings in the app, turn off alert email, App Notifications or texts on the Alerts screen, use the unsubscribe link in any alert email, or reply STOP to any GoSki text (HELP for help). You can turn off location or notifications for GoSki in your phone's Settings, and remove GoSki's access in your Apple or Google account settings. Text alerts are also covered by our Terms of Use.

6. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections and access controls.

7. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or data requests: goskiapp@gmail.com. Help with signing in, GoSki PRO and deleting your account is on our Support page.
