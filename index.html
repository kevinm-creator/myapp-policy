Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: October 17, 2026

GoSki is operated by RAK Industries LLC, a Connecticut company. This policy explains what the GoSki app collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Use

GoSki uses your first and last name, email address and account identifier to verify your email, identify your account and provide account features. We store the resorts you save as favorites and the resort requests you submit so those features work across sessions.

If you pick ski regions (up to two regions, or some of their states), we store your choice, or that you skipped the question, with your account so the Explore screen can show snow forecasts, storm news and resort updates for those areas on any phone you sign in to. Picking regions is optional and does not use your device's location. We may count how many accounts picked each region, without names or email addresses, to decide which resorts to add.

If you tell us which ski passes you hold, or that you don't have one, we store that with your account so the app can show each pass's blackout dates and deals at the resorts it covers. Choosing a pass is optional.

If you turn on snow alerts, we store the resorts you follow for alerts and your alert choices: Snow Alert, storm warnings and snow forecast updates, the snowfall amount that triggers a forecast alert, quiet hours, whether alert email is on, and your phone's time zone setting so quiet hours follow your local time. We keep a record of each alert prepared for you and whether it was delivered for 90 days, so the same alert is not sent twice and delivery problems can be fixed. Alerts go to the ways you pick: your account's email address, App Notifications on your iPhone, or both; or, if you choose text messages, to your mobile phone instead of email.

If you choose App Notifications, we store each iPhone's notification token from Apple, whether it belongs to a test or App Store copy of the app, the app version and when the app last updated it, and a record of each notification sent to each iPhone with Apple's message ID. We keep a notification token until you sign out on that iPhone, turn App Notifications off, delete your account, or Apple reports that the token no longer works. Send records are normally removed after about 90 days.

If you choose text-message alerts, we store your mobile phone number, the date and time you agreed to receive texts and the wording you agreed to, and whether texts are on or stopped (for example, because you replied STOP). We use your number only to send the GoSki alerts you asked for and the replies to your HELP and STOP messages.

When you purchase or restore a pass, we process purchase records, product identifiers, subscription status and account identifiers to verify access, recognize renewals and refunds, and understand subscription performance. These records can be linked to your GoSki account. Apple handles payment details; GoSki does not receive your payment card number.

2. Service Providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication and store app and account data. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain access to paid features and provide subscription analytics. These providers process the information needed for those services. Email delivery providers process your address and the messages we send you (sign-in codes and, if you turn them on, snow alert emails) to deliver them, and tell us when a message bounces or is reported as spam. If you choose text-message alerts, Twilio (with Amazon Web Services as a backup) and mobile carriers process your phone number and the messages we send you to deliver them, and tell us when you reply STOP or HELP. If you choose App Notifications, Apple's push notification service receives your iPhone's notification token and each notification's text to deliver it. Apple processes App Store purchases under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your consent to receive text messages with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the providers named above that deliver our messages.

Snow alerts are based on public forecasts and alerts from the U.S. National Weather Service and, for Canadian resorts, Environment and Climate Change Canada. GoSki requests that information for resort locations and does not send either agency any information about you.

GoSki does not sell your personal information or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising. Choosing a resort, saving a favorite or following a resort for alerts does not give GoSki access to your device's precise location.

3. Location, Ski Recordings and Offline Maps

When you start a ski session and grant permission, GoSki records precise GPS location and available pressure measurements to estimate your route, elevation, speed and ski-day statistics. Recording can continue while the screen is locked. Pause or Finish stops session capture.

Recording journals are encrypted on your device and associated with the account that recorded them. GoSki does not upload these journals or provide cloud recording backup. You can export a recording; exported files are shared only to the destination you choose. Finished recordings can be deleted using their export and manage controls. Deleting your account also erases that account's recordings saved on the phone you delete it from, so export any you want to keep first.

Downloaded map areas and planned routes are stored locally and have their own management and deletion controls. Map and weather services receive requests for the geographic areas you view or download. Those requests are separate from your ski recording journal. Files you export are not removed when you delete a recording or your account.

4. Account, Alert and Purchase Choices

You can remove saved favorites in the app, and change or clear your ski regions in Settings, and your ski pass from the You tab. The account deletion option in Settings deletes your GoSki authentication account and its owned profile, ski regions, ski passes, favorites, resort requests, snow alert settings and records, iPhone notification registrations, and its recordings saved on that phone. You can also contact us about account data or deletion using the support address below.

You can turn off alert email or App Notifications, or stop following a resort, on the app's Alerts screen at any time. You can also turn off GoSki notifications in your iPhone's Settings. For email, you can use the unsubscribe option in any alert email (most mail apps show it as an Unsubscribe button). If an alert email bounces permanently or is reported as spam, we turn off alert email for that account.

If you get alerts by text message, you can switch to email or turn texts off on the Alerts screen at any time, or reply STOP to any GoSki text to stop all GoSki texts. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies, up to 6 messages a day, and message and data rates may apply. Deleting your account deletes your phone number and text-message settings. Text alerts are covered by our Terms of Use.

Deleting your GoSki account does not cancel an App Store subscription or erase Apple's purchase history. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are separate from your GoSki profile and may remain for purchase validation, restoration and transaction administration. Contact us with questions about those records.

We keep account information to operate your account and saved features until it is removed through these controls. We may update this policy as GoSki changes; the date above identifies the latest update.
