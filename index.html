Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Use

GoSki uses your first and last name, email address and account identifier to verify your email, identify your account and provide account features. We store the resorts you save as favorites and the resort requests you submit so those features work across sessions.

When you purchase or restore a pass, we process purchase records, product identifiers, subscription status and account identifiers to verify access, recognize renewals and refunds, and understand subscription performance. These records can be linked to your GoSki account. Apple handles payment details; GoSki does not receive your payment card number.

2. Service Providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication and store app and account data. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain access to paid features and provide subscription analytics. These providers process the information needed for those services. Email delivery providers process your address and sign-in messages to deliver verification emails. Apple processes App Store purchases under its own privacy policy.

GoSki does not sell your personal information or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising. Choosing a resort or saving a favorite does not give GoSki access to your device's precise location.

3. Account and Purchase Choices

You can remove saved favorites in the app. The account deletion option in Settings deletes your GoSki authentication account and its owned profile, favorites and resort requests. You can also contact us about account data or deletion using the support address below.

Deleting your GoSki account does not cancel an App Store subscription or erase Apple's purchase history. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are separate from your GoSki profile and may remain for purchase validation, restoration and transaction administration. Contact us with questions about those records.

We keep account information to operate your account and saved features until it is removed through these controls. We may update this policy as GoSki changes; the date above identifies the latest update.
