Privacy Policy Support

GoSki Support

Email: goskiapp@gmail.com

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours. Please include the email address you sign in with and a short description of what happened.

Signing in

  • GoSki signs you in with a 6-digit code sent to your email. There is no password.
  • The code comes from Verification@goskiapp.com. If it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder, then ask for a new code.
  • Codes expire after a few minutes. Always use the newest code you received.

GoSki Pass

  • Weekly and monthly GoSki Pass subscriptions are billed by Apple through your App Store account and renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Restore your Pass: sign in to the same GoSki account, open the GoSki Pass screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your GoSki account does not cancel a subscription. Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: request a refund from Apple.

Delete your account

  • In the app, open the You tab, tap the settings button next to your name, then tap Delete Account.
  • This erases your sign-in, profile, favorites, snow alerts, resort requests and the account's ski recordings saved on that phone. Export any recordings you want to keep first.
  • Can't open the app? Email goskiapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account.

Snow alert emails

  • Turn alert email off or stop following a resort on the app's Snow alerts screen.
  • You can also tap Unsubscribe in any alert email.

Resort conditions and maps

  • Snowfall, lift and trail status come from each resort's own reports and change when the resort updates them.
  • Trail maps and ski-day statistics are estimates. Always follow posted resort signs and ski patrol.
